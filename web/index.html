the architecture time machine

strata rebuilds the module dependency graph at every commit in a repository, then lets you scrub through the result. Hover a module to isolate its neighbourhood; click it for its history, dependencies and size.

keys

←→ step through commits space play / pause f fit graph esc close

reading it

  • One circle is one directory. Size grows with lines of code.
  • An arrow means "imports": the source depends on the target.
  • red nodes and edges form a dependency cycle.
  • New modules and edges flash white on the commit that introduced them.
  • Layer colours come from .strata.json; without it, colours are stable by path.

strata stores commit hashes, dates and subjects. It never reads or stores author identities, and the server listens on localhost by default.
